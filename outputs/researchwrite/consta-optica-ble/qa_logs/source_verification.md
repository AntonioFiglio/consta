## Drafting conditions and source corrections

1. **Institutional versus independent:** Johns Hopkins and NHS institutional publications verify what participants report; they are primary, not automatically independent of the deployment. European Sony involvement must stay visible. This review independently checks retrieval/interpretation, not clinical results.
2. **No blanket approval:** Replace any suggestion of already-approved Consta corridors with “áreas a aprovar” or “em áreas autorizadas após avaliação”. There is no hospital authorization in this record.
3. **No causal overstatement:** US pump redistribution is a combined work-system intervention. UK search-time savings are programme-reported estimates. Sweden's search-time outcome is based on staff perceptions/surveys. None supplies a Consta forecast.
4. **NHS terminology is not a taxonomy authority:** The Mid Cheshire page loosely calls barcodes passive tags and uses “triangulate” for signal-strength positioning. Explain mechanisms using the technical sources, not those labels. Scanned passive inventory remains distinct from autonomous mobile tracking.
5. **Technology specificity:** Keep RF+IR, BLE RSS and AoA separate. Radboud's institutional source does not identify its exact radio; do not fill it in from supplier/general product descriptions.
6. **Passive optics has a scope:** A printed marker can avoid a powered transmitter on the asset. Cameras, cabling, lighting, installation and field-of-view still require evaluation. Passive marker identity is not clinical safety or guaranteed visibility.
7. **Emulation evidence is pending a separate audit:** `01_research_canon.md` mentions result artifacts, but this source-verification review has not opened their contents or verified their conclusions. Drafted numerical results require the actual experiment/independent verification artifact and its constraints, not the source register.
8. **Access limitations are material:** Retain the publisher-403 disclosure for Sweden and the limited direct recheck of HAMILTON. Successful targeted index retrieval supports stated mechanisms; it does not prove a complete journal-paper audit.

## Critical issues

No source contradiction requiring scope reset was found in the foundation. The highest-risk editorial errors would be calling all cases BLE, describing cloud-path cases as offline, treating “outside MRI” as safety clearance, or labeling the optical emulator a proven hospital detector. The existing canon forbids these; enforcement is required during drafting and final QA.
